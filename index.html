Notated — the rules for this box

Written down from the walkthrough, so every game that uses this box follows them.

  • 1Pixelization first. Walking along, a monster appears — the screen pixelates into the battle, then the box is already there.
  • 2Quick options open in place. Tap Attack and the choices open inside the box. Nobody scrolls down the middle of the screen to read.
  • 3Four choices, or type your own. Every question gives 4 answers to tap — plus a box to type a different answer.
  • 4Short messages only. What happened shows as one big line up top — “Poison!”, “Healing Wind” — never a long paragraph.
  • 5HP · MP · Limit · Time, always visible. Names on the left, numbers on the right. When Limit fills up, the Limit Break — the superpower — unlocks.
  • 6The box never takes over the screen. It stays at the bottom, sized so the fight is always in view.
  • 7It has to be Nova's turn first. Attack does NOT pop up right away. Nova's TIME bar fills, the “NOVA'S TURN” marker drops over him, a banner calls his turn — only then does the box open.
  • 8You control Nova only. Sage and Pip are friends Nova found on the journey. They attack on their own timers — no old-school controlling everybody.
  • 9PSN means Poison. The purple PSN tag = poisoned. It ticks −9 HP again and again while Nova chooses — that's the “keeps dying by nine” bug, explained. Item → Antidote cures it.
  • 10Magic = bigger hits. Limit = superpower. Magic costs MP for a harder question and a bigger hit. Limit stays greyed out until the bar is 100% full, then it unlocks as a rainbow Limit Break.
